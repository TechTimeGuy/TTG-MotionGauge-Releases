# Support

Official help and support information: https://techtimeguy.com/motiongauge/support/

For a private support request, email sales@techtimeguy.com. Optional financial support is handled only through the official support page, is not required to use the application, and does not unlock features or create a software license.

When requesting help, include:

- TTG MotionGauge version;
- Windows version and architecture;
- telemetry format (SRT, GPX, or FIT);
- export mode, profile, and selected encoder;
- whether the problem occurs in preview, burned-in export, or transparent export;
- the exact error message; and
- concise reproduction steps.

Do not publicly share original video, GPS tracks, telemetry files, MapTiler API keys, signing credentials, private gauge artwork, or layout exports without reviewing them first. GPS telemetry can reveal precise locations and travel history. Screenshots may also expose filenames, map locations, or private footage.

For export problems, state whether FFmpeg is available on PATH or through another local installation. Do not upload copyrighted codecs or third-party binaries to a support request.
